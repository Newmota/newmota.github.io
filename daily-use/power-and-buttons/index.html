    Relays, Buttons & LEDs¶

    Switching outputs on and off, timers that turn things off automatically, how buttons behave, and what your relays do after a power cut. Assumes your pins are already assigned — see Setting Up Your Device Hardware if a relay doesn't react. For dimming, color, and PWM lights, see Lights & PWM.

    Power Commands¶

    Command Syntax Description
    Power / Power<n> Power [0\|1\|2\|3\|4\|on\|off\|toggle\|blink\|blinkoff] Controls or queries logical relay and light power states.
    Power0 Power0 [0\|1\|2\|on\|off\|toggle] Broadcasts a power state change to all configured outputs simultaneously.
    PowerOnState PowerOnState [0..5] Sets the boot-up power policy for relays.
    PulseTime PulseTime<n> [0..64900] Configures an automatic duration timer for relay <n>.
    BlinkTime BlinkTime [1..36000] Configures the blink cycle period (in tenths of a second).
    BlinkCount BlinkCount [0..65535] Configures the number of blink cycles before stopping.
    Interlock Interlock [0\|1\|groups] Configures mutual exclusion between relays with break-before-make.

    Power State Transitions¶

    Output numbers are 1-based (Power1, Power2, etc.). On single-relay devices, Power and Power1 are interchangeable.

    When both relays and PWM Lights are configured, Newmota preserves relay identities and numbers Light endpoints after the highest configured physical relay index (e.g. Relays at Power1 and Power2 $\rightarrow$ Light at Power3).

    > Power1 on
{"POWER1":"ON"}

> Power1 toggle
{"POWER1":"OFF"}

> Power0 on
{"POWER1":"ON","POWER2":"ON"}

> Power1 blink
{"POWER1":"BLINK"}

> Power1 blinkoff
{"POWER1":"BLINKOFF"}

    Boot-Up Behavior (PowerOnState)¶

    PowerOnState controls the relay state after power restoration or reboot:

    Value Mode Description
    0 Keep Off Relays remain OFF after boot.
    1 Turn On Relays turn ON after boot.
    2 Toggle Saved Inverts the previously saved state after boot.
    3 Restore Saved Restores the exact relay state from before shutdown/restart (Default).
    4 Always On Relays turn ON and reject local command deactivation.
    5 PulseTime Inverse Relays boot OFF, then execute their inverted PulseTime transition.

    Safe Boot Sequencing¶

    When the microcontroller powers on, all relay and LED GPIO outputs are configured logically OFF before applying any restored or policy state, eliminating dangerous power surges or relay chatter.

    Timers & Relay Interlocking¶

    PulseTime (Automatic Shutoff)¶

    PulseTime sets an automatic timer on relay <n>: - Values 1 to 111: Represents duration in tenths of a second (0.1 s to 11.1 s). - Values 112 to 64900: Represents duration in seconds plus 100 (e.g. 112 = 12 seconds, 220 = 120 seconds). - Value 0: Disables PulseTime.

    Interlock (Mutual Relay Exclusion)¶

    Enables mutual exclusion so only one relay in a group can be active at a time (e.g. for motorized blinds or directional motors): - Uses a hardware 50 ms break-before-make delay to protect motor windings and relay contacts from simultaneous activation. - Supports configuring up to 16 disjoint interlock groups (e.g. Interlock 1,2 3,4).

    Physical I/O Drivers (basic_io.cpp)¶

    Newmota's basic I/O driver natively translates logical states to physical pin configurations:

    • Relays: Supports standard active-high (Relay) and active-low inverted (Relay_i) pins.
    • Status LEDs: Supports standard active-high (Led) and active-low inverted (Led_i) indicators, plus an optional network link LED (LedLink, LedLink_i). See Status LEDs.
    • Buttons:
    • Supports pull-up (Button_u), pull-down (Button_d), and inverted (Button_i) buttons.
    • Probed every 10 ms; the filter and handler time comes from ButtonDebounce (default 50 ms, range 40..1000).
    • 4-Second Startup Guard: Ignores button inputs for 4 seconds after boot to prevent accidental resets caused by supply rail transients. Guarded presses are not queued.
    • SetOption Compatibility: Supports SetOption11 (single/double press swap for 2-relay devices), SetOption13 (immediate button response), and SetOption73 (relay decoupling).
    • Values ending in 9 select Tasmota's native AC pulse detector, which Newmota does not implement; they are rejected rather than silently changing behavior. Cooperative probes can also be delayed by blocking application work.

    Switch inputs¶

    Optional USE_SWITCHES (on for applications) reads Switch, Switch_n and Switch_pd GPIOs (Tasmota GPIO_SWT1, _NP, _PD, 32 instances) through the same 10 ms filter as buttons. SwitchDebounce has the ButtonDebounce range and flags and also rejects values ending in 9. SwitchMode<x> 0..16 ports Tasmota's switch handler: the default 0 toggles Power on every change, 1 and 2 follow the level (a pressed, LOW switch is OFF in mode 1), 3–7 act as push buttons, 8–12 add multi-change and hold gestures, 13 and 14 only switch on, 15 and 16 only report. SwitchMode0 reads all 32 or sets them all; SetOption32 sets the hold time. Modes 11 and 12 need several handler passes per hold; with a long SwitchDebounce and a short SetOption32 their INCREMENT repeat stops, as in Tasmota. The level at startup is the switch's last state, so a restart never switches Power; a change during the four-second startup guard acts once the guard ends. Every key logs SWT: Switch1 State 2. SetOption114 1 detaches all switches from Power and publishes {"Switch1":{"Action":"ON"}} on stat RESULT (or SWITCH1 with SetOption4 1) unless SetOption19 1.

    SwitchTopic <topic> sends switch keys to that topic as cmnd/<topic>/POWER<x>, like ButtonTopic (0 disables, 1 copies the device topic, 2 selects the disabled default); keys are indexed by switch number, and a connected route replaces local Power unless SetOption61 1. As in Tasmota, extended gestures (INCREMENT, INV, CLEAR, RELEASE, 100, DELAYED) publish the ON word; SetOption114 Actions carry the gesture itself. SwitchRetain ON|OFF retains these publications; OFF clears the retained keys. SENSOR and Status 8 begin with "Switch1":"ON" for each configured switch, the level flipped by the _INV modes, and modes 15 and 16 publish SENSOR on every change. SwitchText<x> (1–28, at most 15 characters without quotes or backslashes, spaces removed, a leading " clears) replaces Switch<x> in SENSOR and in Action messages; SwitchText lists all 28. Safeboot excludes switches and their commands but keeps their settings.

    See TuyaMCU for switches reported by an external MCU.

    Status LEDs¶

    Discrete LEDs (Led1–Led4, normal or inverted) and the optional LedLink follow Tasmota's rules. Assign them as GPIO functions in your template (for example 288 is an active-high Led1, 320 active-low, and 544 an active-high LedLink; see Setting Up Your Device Hardware).

    Command Description
    LedState 0..7 (default 1) Selects what the LEDs show: bit 0 is the Power state (filtered by LedMask), bit 1 received MQTT messages, bit 2 published MQTT messages.
    LedMask Which power outputs the LEDs follow; decimal or 0x hexadecimal, default 0xFFFF.
    LedPower<n> 0/1/2 Sets an LED manually and turns Power indication off for it. Without a LedLink only LedPower1 exists.
    LedPwmMode<n> 0/1/2 Drives an LED through PWM instead of on/off, using LedPwmOn and LedPwmOff. Without a LedLink only LedPwmMode1 exists.
    LedPwmOn, LedPwmOff Brightness 0..255 for the on and off state (defaults 255 and 0), mapped through the Light gamma curve.
    SetOption31 1 Stops the problem blinks described below.
    • Which LED shows what: the link LED (or Led1 when no LedLink is configured) is the status LED. Without LedLink, Led2 (or Led1 alone) shows Power; with LedLink, LedN mirrors PowerN.
    • Problem blinks: the status LED blinks every second while Wi-Fi is down and every two seconds while enabled MQTT is not connected, and stays lit during a restart or update.
    • PWM LEDs share the PWM carrier with Light outputs (PWMFrequency); a pad without a PWM route stays plain on/off.
    • LedState, LedMask, and the three PWM values persist across restarts.

    Telemetry Integration (Status 11 / StatusSTS)¶

    Configured relay states are reported in Status 11 telemetry:

    > Status 11
{"StatusSTS":{"POWER1":"ON","POWER2":"OFF"}}
