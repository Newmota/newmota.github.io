    Your first hour¶

    1. Build and install¶

      Pick your chip and features with make config and build the image, then back up and flash the device.

    2. Connect to Wi-Fi¶

      Join the setup network or configure Wi-Fi over serial, then open the device in your browser.

    3. Configure the hardware¶

      Map the pins to relays, buttons and lights. Try Power1 on on a relay or Dimmer 50 on a light.
